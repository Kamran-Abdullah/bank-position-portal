How the Review Queue works

  1. When you upload a statement, the system reads it, finds the latest statement date and the closing balance for that date, and scores its confidence.
  2. If confidence is at or above the threshold (Settings, default 75%) and the file matches the bank/account it was uploaded against, the balance posts straight to the dashboard — that account turns 🟢 Done.
  3. Otherwise nothing is posted. The upload lands here as 🟡 Review Required (or 🔴 Processing Error). Typical reasons: the file looks like a different bank, no closing-balance label and the running-balance chain was ambiguous, an unreadable/scanned PDF, or the extracted figure disagreed with opening + credits − debits.
  4. Click Resolve on a row. You see what the system read, and can open the original file. Then either:
    • Confirm — leave the balance field blank to accept the figure the system extracted.
    • Correct — type the right balance and a reason. The original extraction is never overwritten; the correction is recorded (who, when, why) for audit.
  5. On confirm, the balance posts to the dashboard for that statement date, the account turns 🟢 Done, and company / group totals update. The row leaves this queue.
